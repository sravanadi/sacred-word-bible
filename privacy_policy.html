Privacy Policy for Sacred Word

Last Updated: June 4, 2026

sravan abhishek adi ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Sacred Word mobile application (the "App") under the package name com.aistudio.sacredword.vuxwps. We are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

A. Personal Data

Our App does not require any registration, sign-up, or profile creation. We do not collect, request, or store any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address).

B. User-Generated Content (Bookmarks, Highlights, Notes, and Prayers)

All content you create within the App—including bookmarks, highlighted verses, personal notes, and prayers—is stored locally on your device inside a private SQLite/Room database.

  • This data is never uploaded to our servers, shared with third parties, or accessed by us.
  • You can delete your notes, highlights, bookmarks, and prayers at any time directly through the App interface. Uninstalling the App will permanently delete all locally stored content.

C. Network Data & Third-Party APIs

To fetch and display Bible translations, the App makes network requests to public interfaces:

  • HelloAo Bible API (https://bible.helloao.org)
  • API.Bible (https://api.scripture.api.bible)

These requests are used solely to fetch text content dynamically. No personal identifiers or usage tracking data is sent to these endpoints.

2. Advertising (Third-Party Services)

Our App uses advertising to support its development. We integrate Google AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob may collect and use information to personalize and measure ad performance.

These third-party providers may collect:

  • Device Identifiers: Advertising ID (e.g., Google Advertising ID - AAID), IP address, device model, and operating system version.
  • Cookies & Tracking Technology: Cookies may be used to deliver relevant ads to you.

For more information on how Google uses your data, please review:

  • Google Privacy & Terms Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

3. Data Protection and Retention

Because all your personal application data is stored locally on your device:

  • We do not host your personal data on external servers.
  • You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device to protect your stored highlights, notes, and prayers.

4. Children’s Privacy

Our App is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided a third party with personal data through our App, please contact the third-party service provider directly.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy document. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
